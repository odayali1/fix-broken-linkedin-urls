# No match, no charge

You do not pay for a LinkedIn URL that cannot be recovered.

## What the row says

`result` is `No match, free`. `matchType` is `none`. `charge` is `Free`. `currentUrl` is empty. The same columns exist so the file still lines up with the rows that recovered.

A failed request is also free. You pay when a live profile comes back: `exact`, `confident`, or `likely`.

## Why that matters on an old list

Stale CRM exports, old ATS links, and last year's conference lists contain a mix of dead slugs and people who only changed their custom URL. On stale lists, recovery is often up to 70%. The rest of the file costs nothing.

At the Gold price of $1 per 1,000 live profiles, 10,000 old URLs that recover 7,000 profiles cost $7. The 3,000 misses are free.
